What it does

Structure. Polish. Check. Challenge.

Structure

Your thesis as a reader will hear it, and where the narrative loses them.

Polish

Fuzzy sentences, hedges, filler. A tighter phrasing inside your own sentence, in your register. Never a rewrite.

Check

Every factual claim verified with sources: supported, contradicted, or needs precision.

Challenge

The strongest counterargument, the blind spots, the gaps.

It never writes for you. It shows you the gap. A suggestion can only replace the words it quotes, stays close to their length, and keeps your register. Nothing changes in your draft until you click Apply.

In the composer

How it works

  1. A small badge appears on the compose window

    Nothing is read or sent until you click it. Auto-analyze at 40 words is a setting, off by default.

  2. Click to analyze

    Findings appear as highlights over your text, each with a hover card, and a Challenges panel docks beside the draft with the open claims and the counterargument.

  3. Apply or Keep

    A suggestion shows a diff of the quoted span only. Apply writes it into the editor through the host's own undo stack, so Ctrl+Z works. Keep leaves your wording and dismisses the finding.

  4. Re-analyze when you want

    Editing shifts the highlights with your text and marks touched findings stale. Nothing runs again until you press Re-analyze. Only changed paragraphs and unverified claims are sent.

Privacy

Your draft goes to your provider and nowhere else

Your draft goes to the provider you chose, from your own account, and nowhere else. No WordSnap server, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account.

Works with your own OpenRouter account, connected with one click, running GLM 5.2. You pay OpenRouter, not WordSnap. GLM 5.2 served by Z.AI is the only model this build runs; its prompts and response parsing are validated against it alone. The key is stored locally inside the extension and never reaches a web page: the script that draws over your draft talks only to the extension's own background process.

Fact check and counterargument use your provider's web search, so the claims in your draft are sent as search queries under your account. The privacy policy lists what is handled, what is stored, and why each permission is requested.

Open source

Read the code that reads your draft

WordSnap is released under the Apache-2.0 license at github.com/jalemieux/wordsnap. The rules that keep it honest are enforced in code, not only in prompts: a finding whose quote cannot be located in your text is dropped, a cited URL that did not appear in that request's search results is dropped, and a suggestion longer than 1.3 times the span it replaces is rejected.

Bugs and questions go to GitHub issues.
