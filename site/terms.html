Your provider account

WordSnap sends your draft to the language model provider you connect, OpenRouter or Anthropic, using your own account. You are responsible for that account, for its terms, and for any costs it incurs. WordSnap does not bill you and cannot see your usage.

The analysis can be wrong

Findings, verdicts, confidence levels, suggested wording and counterarguments are produced by a language model to help you judge your own writing. They can be mistaken, incomplete or out of date. You decide what to send. Nothing is written into your draft unless you click Apply change.

Sources shown beside a finding come from the provider's web search. Check them before relying on them.
